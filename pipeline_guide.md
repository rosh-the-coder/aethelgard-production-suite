# Aethelgard Art Co. — Pipeline Guide

This guide outlines the step-by-step process to run your automated digital art production pipeline from end-to-end. Use it to generate high-resolution prints, build mockup previews, compile download sheets, and upload drafts to Etsy.

---

## 🚀 End-to-End Walkthrough

### Step 1: Niche Selection & Market Research
1. Open the dashboard at **[http://127.0.0.1:8080](http://127.0.0.1:8080)**.
2. In the **Market Research** tab, under **Validated Niche Presets**, locate the **Moody Dark Academia** card.
3. Click the **Load Preset Prompt** button. 
   * *This will auto-fill your concept (`moody classic barn owl painting, vintage dark academia study`), select the Chiaroscuro style preset, and automatically redirect you to the **Print Generator** tab.*
4. *(Optional)* To do custom research, enter a search term (e.g. "plaster wall art") in the keyword box and hit **Search**. You will see estimated monthly listing sales, revenues ($), and opportunity scores.

---

### Step 2: Generate & Review Candidates
1. On the **Print Generator** tab, review the settings and the compiled spine prompt.
2. Click **Generate Candidate Variations**.
3. **Wait ~30 seconds.** The spinner will run while Imagen 3 renders three candidate variations:
   * **Faithful:** Directly follows your prompt.
   * **Signature:** Applies classic oil-sketch style reference coordinates.
   * **Wildcard:** A creative atmospheric interpretation.
4. Once the candidates load, look at the **AI SEO Suggested Listing Titles** box at the top.
5. Select your favorite candidate by clicking its card (it will highlight gold).
6. Click one of the suggested title pills. 
   * *This will instantly copy the SEO-optimized title into the product title field of your selected card.*
7. Adjust the **Edge Trim Canvas Margin** slider to **3%** (this crops out any rough white paper borders generated by the AI).
8. Click **Finalize Selected Winner**.

---

### Step 3: Inspect Production Assets
You will be redirected to the **Catalog & Uploads** tab:
1. In the **Product Catalog** tree on the left, click on your newly finalized piece.
2. The master print will display on the screen.
3. Look at the thumbnails below the print:
   * Select a static mockup to see your print blended onto the wall frame.
   * Select the thumbnail highlighted in gold with the **ZOOM EFFECT ACTIVE** badge. This will play your animated Ken Burns zoom-in GIF showing off the canvas details.
4. Open your computer's File Explorer and navigate to your workspace directory:
   👉 `F:\Apps\Etsy 2026\roshwillberich\tooling\digital-product-research\artwork-runs\<run_folder>\<piece_folder>\`
   * *You will find the ready-to-sell JPEGs cropped and upscaled at 300 DPI in 4:5, 3:2, and 11:14 aspect ratios, alongside your static mockups and `mockup_zoom.gif`.*

---

### Step 4: Generate Customer Download PDF
Etsy has a 20MB file upload limit. To bypass this, we deliver files using a Google Drive share link embedded inside a PDF download sheet:
1. In the Catalog editor panel on the right, click **Compile PDF**.
2. A browser prompt will ask for your Google Drive folder link.
3. Enter a placeholder link (e.g. `https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/test-owl-print`) and click **OK**.
4. The system will compile the branded PDF and save it in the piece's folder. Under **Piece Settings**, the badge will update to **PDF links generated** (success!).

---

### Step 5: Test Etsy Upload (Risk-Free Draft Mode)
*Creating a draft listing on Etsy is **100% free**—it only costs the €0.20 listing fee if you decide to officially publish it to the public store.*

1. **Authenticate (First time only):** Click **Etsy Authentication** in the top navbar. A browser window will open. Log into your Etsy Seller account, then close the window. (Your session cookies are saved securely locally).
2. **Push to Etsy:** In the Catalog editor panel, click **Upload Draft to Etsy**.
3. Playwright will launch, log in using your session, upload all your mockups (including the animated zoom GIF!), paste your SEO titles, tags, and description, set the price to `$5.99`, and save it as a **Draft**.
4. Open your normal web browser, go to your **Etsy Shop Manager ➔ Listings ➔ Drafts**, and verify that your listing is there.
