Listing Capture Bookmarklet

Phase 1 of EverBee-style listing analysis: grab public listing signals from the page you’re viewing, then estimate the paid-style metrics in the Production Suite.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

Capture Etsy Listing → Suite

  1. Dashboard running at http://127.0.0.1:8080
  2. Open any listing (/listing/... or /ie/listing/...)
  3. Click the bookmarklet
  4. Open Market Research → Listing Analyzer

Views, favorites, age, tags = public. Monthly/total sales, conversion, visibility = estimates (same class as EverBee paid — not private Etsy data).
