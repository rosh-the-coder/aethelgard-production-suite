Shop Capture Bookmarklet

Same idea as EverBee: read the shop page inside your real Chrome, then send the public stats into the Production Suite.

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Capture Etsy Shop → Suite

  1. Keep the dashboard running at http://127.0.0.1:8080
  2. Open the shop Items tab (regional URLs like /ie/shop/... are fine)
  3. Click the bookmarklet
  4. Return to Market Research — capture auto-loads

Replace any older bookmarklet by deleting it and dragging this one again.
